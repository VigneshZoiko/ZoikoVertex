# **1. Executive Doctrine and Product Definition**
The Forensic Hub is the governed investigation layer for ZoikoVertex. It converts audit events, AI actions, approvals, policy triggers, user activity, evidence objects, platform events, and identity signals into structured cases that can be investigated, assigned, reconstructed, preserved, exported, and defended.

Non-negotiable doctrine: if Audit Trail answers "what happened", Forensic Hub answers "what happened, why it matters, what evidence proves it, who is accountable, what risks exist, and what actions are now required."

|**Principle**|**Build Requirement**|
| :- | :- |
|Case-first design|Every serious event must be capable of becoming an investigation case with owner, severity, scope, timeline, evidence set, notes, actions, and closure decision.|
|Deterministic reconstruction|Related timelines must be generated by defined correlation rules and visible confidence levels, not vague AI summaries.|
|Evidence-preserving workflow|Investigators must be able to preserve events into Evidence Vault without losing chain metadata, hashes, timestamps, or actor context.|
|Defensible collaboration|Every comment, assignment, status change, note, redaction, export, and legal-hold action becomes a new audit event.|
|Least-privilege visibility|Investigators see only the fields their role permits. Sensitive values are redacted, hashed, scoped, or denied according to the access matrix.|
|Regulator-ready output|Case exports must produce readable, traceable, tamper-evident packs with event lineage, evidence manifest, chain-of-custody, and closure rationale.|

# **9. Timeline Reconstruction and Causality Rules**
Timeline reconstruction is not a feed. It is a forensic view that must preserve order, causality, authority, and evidence source. The system must separate chronological proximity from causal relationship.

|**Rule**|**Correlation Key**|**Use**|**Confidence**|
| :- | :- | :- | :- |
|Workflow Run|workflow\_run\_id|Reconstruct one end-to-end workflow, from content creation to approval, policy check, publish, export, or failure.|Deterministic|
|Approval Chain|approval\_chain\_id|Show all required, granted, rejected, overridden, or skipped approvals.|Deterministic|
|Content Lineage|object\_id + related\_objects|Trace campaign -> asset -> variant -> platform post -> engagement/message.|High|
|Actor Session|actor\_id + session\_id + time window|Reconstruct user or agent actions during a specific session.|High|
|Policy Rule|policy\_rule\_id + affected\_object|Group all events tied to a triggered, failed, or overridden policy rule.|High|
|Platform Receipt|platform\_event\_id or external\_receipt\_id|Tie ZoikoVertex action to external platform confirmation or failure.|High|
|Temporal Proximity|same actor/object within defined window|Surface possible related events when stronger keys are missing.|Medium|
|AI Similarity Assist|semantic match across event summaries and content snapshots|Suggest potentially relevant items only; never auto-add as proven evidence.|Suggestive|

*Rule: AI-assisted correlation may recommend evidence, but it cannot mark evidence as proven, vault-preserved, or causally linked without human confirmation or deterministic key match.*

# **19. Security, Privacy, and Compliance Controls**
- Tenant isolation must be enforced at database, search index, object storage, queue, and API middleware levels.
- All read responses must pass field-level redaction before serialization.
- External auditors must access approved exports or locked auditor workspaces only, never unrestricted live case navigation by default.
- Legal privileged materials must be compartmentalized and excluded from general exports unless Legal explicitly includes them.
- External evidence uploads require malware scanning, content-type validation, size limits, checksum calculation, and audit logging.
- No case, note, evidence item, export, or legal-hold action may be hard-deleted through application paths.
- Every investigative action must emit an Audit Trail event with actor, timestamp, object, reason, before/after where applicable, and chain metadata.
- Data residency must follow tenant configuration. Cross-region case packages require explicit legal/compliance permission.

# **26. Acceptance Criteria - Full Lock**
1. Forensic Hub exists as a dedicated Evidence Layer module, not a generic reporting page.
1. Cases can be created manually, automatically by rules, and from selected Audit Trail events.
1. Every case has type, severity, owner, status, SLA, source, evidence set, and audit-backed history.
1. Lifecycle state machine is enforced server-side and cannot be bypassed by the UI.
1. Every case action emits an Audit Trail event with actor, timestamp, reason, object, and before/after where applicable.
1. Case queue supports filters, saved views, assignment, SLA visibility, and severity sorting.
1. Case detail workbench includes timeline, evidence, graph, notes, tasks, remediation, and export surfaces.
1. Timeline reconstruction uses deterministic correlation rules with visible confidence labels.
1. AI-assisted evidence suggestions are labeled as suggestions and cannot become proven evidence without human confirmation.
1. Evidence can be pinned, unpinned, preserved, legally held, and exported without deletion of source history.
1. Evidence Vault integration preserves hashes, event IDs, chain metadata, retention class, and manifest ID.
1. Legal hold overrides retention and can be applied only by authorized Legal roles with reason and scope.
1. Privileged notes and materials are compartmentalized from general investigators and exports.
1. Field-level access matrix is enforced across API, UI, exports, timeline, graph, and search.
1. Restricted related events render as opaque timeline items instead of leaking metadata.
1. Severity model and SLA rules are implemented with escalation alerts.
1. Export builder requires reason, scope, redaction profile, approval, manifest, and audit event.
1. Regulator pack includes summary, timeline, evidence manifest, hashes, redaction log, custody trail, and approval proof.
1. External uploads are scanned, checksummed, classified, and audit-logged.
1. No application path hard-deletes cases, notes, evidence links, exports, or legal-hold actions.
1. Tenant isolation is enforced at database, API, search, queue, and storage layers.
1. Data residency follows tenant region and is included in case and export metadata.
1. Performance targets are met or degraded states are visible and actionable.
1. Case package generation supports async processing and resumable delivery for large exports.
1. Observability alerts fire for audit emission failure, vault backlog, export failure, redaction bypass, and SLA breach.
1. Mobile supports review, assignment, notes, escalation, and basic timeline review.
1. Accessibility meets WCAG 2.2 AA across all core surfaces.
1. QA suite validates permissions, state transitions, audit coverage, correlation, vault integration, exports, performance, and security.
1. Security, Compliance, Legal, Product, Design, and Engineering sign off independently before release.
1. The module can withstand a customer CISO, General Counsel, regulator, or board-level audit walkthrough without unsupported claims.
